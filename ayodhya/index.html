Suggested four-day flow

Day by day.

A deliberately relaxed outline. Add exact dates and timings once the travel window is final.

Day 1 · Gorakhpur → Ayodhya

Travel in, settle, then explore

  • Start from Gorakhpur at a family-comfortable time.
  • Check in and take a rest break before sightseeing.
  • Keep the evening for a gentle first visit around Ram Janmabhoomi / Ram Mandir area, subject to current access arrangements.
  • If energy allows, consider Saryu riverfront in the evening.

Avoid packing too many temple visits into the arrival day.

Directions ↗
Day 2 · Ayodhya

Full day for Ayodhya

  • Ram Mandir / Ram Janmabhoomi visit — check official entry guidance before leaving.
  • Hanuman Garhi and Kanak Bhawan, subject to crowd and access conditions.
  • Dashrath Mahal and nearby old-city sights if time permits.
  • Saryu ghat / evening aarti only after confirming the current schedule and crowd arrangements.

Build in meal, hydration and rest breaks; keep one optional attraction as a buffer.

Ayodhya places ↗
Day 3 · Ayodhya → Sultanpur → Lucknow

Two road legs, one flexible stop

  • Leave Ayodhya after breakfast and drive towards Sultanpur.
  • Use Sultanpur as a break / family visit / lunch stop, depending on your purpose there.
  • Continue to Lucknow and check in.
  • Keep the evening light: dinner and rest, or a short local outing.

If Sultanpur is only a transit stop, keep the halt short to protect the Lucknow evening.

Directions ↗
Day 4 · Lucknow → Gorakhpur

Return home

  • Breakfast and checkout.
  • Start for Gorakhpur with meal and washroom breaks planned.
  • Keep a generous buffer for traffic and any onward commitments.

If you want to explore Lucknow properly, consider extending the trip rather than rushing the return.
