  • 5:25 AM: scheduled arrival at Jamnagar

Wednesday · 30 December

Dwarka sightseeing

  • Morning: Nageshwar Jyotirlinga
  • Optional: Bet Dwarka / other local sights
  • Return for lunch and afternoon rest
  • Evening: Shivrajpur Beach
Thursday · 31 December

Dwarka → Somnath

  • Start around 7:00 AM after breakfast
  • Allow 5–6 hours including comfort breaks
  • Hotel check-in, lunch and rest
  • Evening: Somnath Jyotirlinga darshan
  • Night: light & sound show, subject to operating schedule
Friday · 1 January

Somnath → Diu

  • Breakfast and drive to Diu
  • Hotel check-in and rest
  • Afternoon: Diu Fort, St. Paul's Church; Naida Caves / INS Khukri Memorial optional
  • Evening: Nagoa Beach
Saturday · 2 January

Diu → Porbandar → Delhi

  • Early morning: Gangeshwar Mahadev
  • Breakfast and checkout
  • Leave for Porbandar early; allow for road and meal breaks
  • Target station arrival by 3:30–4:00 PM for the planned 7 PM train
  • Confirm exact return train and station before travel
